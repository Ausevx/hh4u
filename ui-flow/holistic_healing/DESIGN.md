## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an Ayurvedic, grounded, and modern Indian holistic wellness philosophy. It balances time-tested restorative rituals with modern mobile behavior. The interface evokes quiet clarity, sanctuary, and mindful progress, avoiding clinical detachment or high-friction mystical cliches.

The visual style merges **Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile & Organic Materiality**:
- **Atmosphere:** Sunlit, organic, calming, and deeply grounded. The tone feels like textured handmade paper bathed in natural morning light.
- **Audience:** Contemporary wellness seekers, mindful practitioners, and individuals looking for stress regulation, holistic lifestyle guidance, and personalized daily routines.
- **Emotional Intent:** Reduces visual fatigue, lowers cognitive load, invites slow and intentional interaction, and instills a sense of continuous care.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical herbalism, terracotta pottery, and natural sunlight, calibrated specifically for mobile daylight viewing and reduced evening eye-strain.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2F5D50` - Deep Forest Green):** Used for focal action elements, prominent app bars, primary buttons, and selected state indicators. Communicates stability and deep vitality.
- **Secondary (`#A8C7B5` - Muted Sage Green):** Used for soft interactive fills, passive state indicators, secondary badges, and progress tracks.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#C8785D` - Muted Terracotta):** Represents earth, warmth, and mindfulness markers. Used for interactive highlights, journaling prompts, callout badges, and reflective moments.
- **Highlight Accent (`#E6B566` - Warm Marigold):** Auspicious sunlight tone used sparingly for morning check-in states, streak completions, and conversational coach highlights.
- **Canvas / Background (`#FFF9F0` - Soft Warm Cream):** Warm, non-glare surface base across all primary screens.
- **Surface / Container (`#EDF5EE` - Pale Eucalyptus):** Elevated surface fill for cards, chat bubbles, list groupings, and bottom sheets.
- **Typography & Details:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#263C34` (Deep Charcoal Green) ensures high accessibility contrast against `#FFF9F0` and `#EDF5EE`.
  - `Text Secondary`: `#6F746E` (Warm Mineral Gray) for metadata, supporting timestamps, and secondary descriptions.
  - `Success`: `#4C8B63` (Leaf Green) for completion badges and healthy metric trackers.
  - `Warning / Critical`: `#B94F45` (Restrained Rust) for missed milestones and cancellation alerts, keeping the tone gentle rather than alarming.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes approachable geometry with open apertures and friendly curves. `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides a contemporary, warm aesthetic that pairs cleanly with regional Indian script systems (such as `Noto Sans Devanagari` when localized).

### Typographic Rules
- **Sentence Case Primacy:** All headers, buttons, chips, and menu items must use sentence case (e.g., "Begin daily reflection", not "BEGIN DAILY REFLECTION"). Sentence case conveys a non-intrusive, supportive dialogue.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy leverages a generous 1.5x to 1.6x line height to support relaxed, sustained reading during guided articles and wellness reflections.
- **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Rely on weight transitions (`700` Display down to `400` Body) and color gradation (`#263C34` for titles, `#6F746E` for secondary notes) rather than high font-size jumps.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an adaptable Android 4-column fluid mobile grid, transitioning to an 8-column layout on foldables and tablets.

### Layout Principles
- **Touch Ergonomics:** Primary actions (chat bar, audio meditation play buttons, entry logging) reside in the bottom 40% of the screen. Touch targets adhere strictly to a minimum bounding box of 48×48dp.
- **Breathing Room:** Screen layouts favor airy margins (`margin` at 20dp/1.25rem) to evoke mindfulness. Elements are never crammed to the edge of the viewport.
- **Rhythm Scale:** Spacing follows an 8dp base unit (with a 4dp micro-step for compact metadata tags and icon pairings).
  - `space-xs` (4dp): Gap between inline icon and label.
  - `space-sm` (8dp): Space between closely related items within cards.
  - `space-md` (16dp): Standard internal container padding and list gaps.
  - `space-lg` (24dp): Gaps separating distinct wellness sections or modules.
  - `space-xl` (36dp): Top canvas clearance and thematic group dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm tonal surfaces combined with diffused organic ambient shadows, avoiding stark artificial black drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** `#FFF9F0` (Soft Warm Cream). Flat background for main screens.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Chat Bubbles):** `#EDF5EE` (Pale Eucalyptus). Outlined optionally with a fine 1dp `#E2ECE4` edge to preserve clarity without harsh borders.
- **Tier 2 (Modals, Bottom Sheets, Quick Action Cards):** Pure `#FFFFFF` tinted with 2% `#2F5D50`. Elevated with an ambient drop shadow:
  - `Y: 4dp`, `Blur: 16dp`, `Spread: 0dp`, `Color: rgba(38, 60, 52, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Action & Meditation Controller):** Tinted floating container:
  - `Y: 8dp`, `Blur: 24dp`, `Spread: -2dp`, `Color: rgba(38, 60, 52, 0.12)`.

### Botanical Accents & Watermarks
Subtle botanical line motifs (such as neem, tulsi, and lotus contours) are applied as single-color stroke watermarks at 6% opacity `#2F5D50` within card backdrops, preserving high legibility.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, natural rounding to reinforce comfort and fluidity.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 8dp / 0.5rem):** Text fields, mini chips, step trackers.
- **Primary Content Cards & Dialogs (`rounded-lg`, 16dp / 1rem):** Journaling modules, wellness habit cards, lifestyle insight blocks.
- **Hero Containers & Bottom Sheets (`rounded-xl`, 24dp / 1.5rem):** Top corners of bottom navigation sheets, expanded media players, floating modal dialogs.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Floating primary action buttons, wellness category filters, and message pill inputs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped, minimum height 48dp. Background: `#2F5D50`, text: `#FFF9F0`, typography: `label-lg`. Pressed state applies a slight scale down (0.98) and color shift to `#24493F`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background with 1.5dp border `#2F5D50` or `#A8C7B5`. Text: `#2F5D50`.
- **Accent Button (Journal Commit / Ritual Completion):** Terracotta `#C8785D` background with white text, used for milestone interactions.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Input:** Min-height 52dp. Background: `#EDF5EE`, border: 1dp solid `#D9E7DD`. Focus border switches to 1.5dp `#2F5D50`. Rounded radius: 12dp. Placeholder text: `#6F746E`. Includes generous 16dp horizontal internal padding.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection Chips:** Pill-shaped, height 36dp. Inactive state: `#EDF5EE` background, `#263C34` text, no border. Active state: `#2F5D50` background, `#FFF9F0` text with smooth 200ms background transition.

### Cards & Habit Containers
- Built on `#EDF5EE` surfaces with 16dp corner radius and 16dp internal padding. Can feature an optional top-right botanical accent motif rendered in `#2F5D50` at 6% opacity.

### Conversational Wellness Assistant (Chat)
- **User Bubble:** Background `#2F5D50`, text `#FFF9F0`, 16dp corner radius with the bottom-right corner tucked to 4dp.
- **Assistant Bubble:** Background `#EDF5EE`, text `#263C34`, 16dp corner radius with the bottom-left corner tucked to 4dp. Border: 1dp solid `#E0EDE3`.
- **Suggested Prompt Bubbles:** Horizontal scrolling row of pill chips tinted with `#A8C7B5` at 20% opacity with `#263C34` text.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular and rounded square toggles with a minimum 48dp touch footprint. Checked state fills with `#2F5D50` with a clean `#FFF9F0` check icon. Unchecked border uses 2dp `#A8C7B5`.

### Progress Rings & Daily Ritual Trackers
- Circular stroke rings using `#A8C7B5` as the unfilled track base and `#2F5D50` (or accent `#E6B566` for morning sunlight streaks) as the active progress stroke, ended with rounded caps.
